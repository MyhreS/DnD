C&S ITEM COST — full transcription
Source: "Item Cost.pdf" (Beta Release), 2 pages (verified with pdfinfo).

Provenance: written by the game maker specifically for the app. The Core
Rulebook states "The app is the authoritative source for prices" and ships no
price list of its own; this document supplies those prices. It is NOT a Codex
download source — it is a data input, and prices surface through the item
catalogue and the shop instead.

Transcribed by hand from the page images: the tables are laid out in two
columns and the text layer interleaves them.

Source typos preserved verbatim and marked [sic]: "Bulllets" (three l's), and
Thieves' Tools weight printed as "1 b." rather than "1 lb.".

Page boundaries are marked [page N].
