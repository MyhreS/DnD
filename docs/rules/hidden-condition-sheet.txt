Hidden Condition Sheet

The Second Threshold. When an Insane hunter’s current Madness equals or exceeds twice their Max Sanity, the
hunter is taken by the outer dark. The GM takes control of the character, and the character is replaced by an Old
One Vessel stat block appropriate to the hunter’s level. All the Old One Vessel forms are listed in the Bestiary
inside The C&S app under Player Alterations.
The Vessel is hostile to all creatures, including the party. It is not the hunter. It is something using the hunter as a
wound through which to enter the world.
If the Vessel is reduced to 0 Hit Points, the hunter returns with 1 Hit Point. Their current Madness becomes equal
to their original Max Sanity, and their Max Sanity permanently becomes twice its original value.
If the same hunter becomes an Old One Vessel a second time, the hunter is lost forever.

Old One Vessel Rules. When a hunter becomes an Old One Vessel, use the stat block matching the hunter’s level
found in the Bestiary inside The C&S app under Player Alterations.
The Vessel replaces the hunter’s game statistics. It does not use the hunter’s weapons, armor, Rites, class
features, feats, transformations, items, or proficiencies unless a rule specifically says otherwise.
The GM controls the Vessel. It is hostile to all creatures.
If the Vessel is reduced to 0 Hit Points, the hunter returns with 1 Hit Point and the Old One Vessel Condition
removed.. Their current Madness becomes equal to their original Max Sanity, and their Max Sanity permanently
becomes twice its original value.
If the same hunter gains the Old One Vessel Condition a second time, that hunter will have that condition
permanently, and is practically removed as a player.

Lost. If you roll Lost on the Transformation Table, you become a Lost: Greater Dreadblood. When a hunter
becomes Lost, they transform into one of the Lost Dreadblood forms listed in the Bestiary inside The C&S app
under Player Alterations. The GM controls the creature.

Lost Rules. When a hunter becomes Lost, use the stat block matching the hunter’s level found in the Bestiary
inside The C&S app under Player Alterations.
The Lost replaces the hunter’s game statistics. It does not use the hunter’s weapons, armor, Rites, class features,
feats, transformations, items, or proficiencies unless a rule specifically says otherwise.
The GM controls the Lost. It is hostile to all creatures.
If the Lost is reduced to 0 Hit Points, the hunter returns with 1 Hit Point and the Lost Condition removed, and
gains 10 + 2d4 Madness minus the players wisdom, modifier.
